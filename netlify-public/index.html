What guides us

Purpose in every part of the school day.

Our values shape how we teach, care for and inspire every learner at PurpleStars.

01 · Our Mission

Care that gives every learner room to grow.

To provide a caring, safe, and intellectually stimulating environment rooted in Christian values, where every learner grows in wisdom, creativity and confidence.

02 · Our Curriculum

Learning children understand, apply and enjoy.

We deliver learning through themes, projects, real-life experiences, creative tasks and technology. Children don’t just memorize — they understand, apply and enjoy learning.

03 · Child's Care

Safety, health and wellbeing built into every day.

A safe and well-equipped environment with a fully functional sickbay, partnerships with qualified paediatricians and hospitals, hygienic restrooms with regular sanitation, and access to clean water and reliable power.

04 · Our Vision

To raise global stars of character, competence, and purpose.
